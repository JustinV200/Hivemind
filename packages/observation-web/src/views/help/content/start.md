The Observation Hive is a live window onto a running Hive: every machine it works on, every bee's
thinking, how capacity is divided, what is waiting on the Queen, and what the Hive has learned. It
is read-only with one exception, the [Chat](#/chat), where you ask the Queen for work and answer
her questions.

## Everything is live

No screen polls and nothing needs a refresh. A view subscribes to the Hive's streams and redraws
as frames arrive. The word beside the dot in the top bar says whether those streams are connected:
`live`, `connecting`, `reconnecting` or `offline`. If a stream drops, the summary strip and the
open view say `stale since` a time, in words, so old numbers are never taken for current ones.

## The words

The Hive borrows its vocabulary from bees, and the screens use those words literally: Queen,
Warden, Cell, Forage, Honey, Capping. Every one is explained in [Words](#/help/glossary), and each
screen's title row carries a one-line gloss with a link into this help. The search box above finds
any word or phrase across every help page, and [Ask](#/help/ask) answers a question in plain words
from these pages, with the sections it read.

- [Thoughts](#/thoughts/queen): what a bee decided and why.

- [Chat](#/chat): talk to the Queen.

- [Cost](#/cost): what the Hive has spent on models.
- [Capping](#/capping): the change gate.
- [Devices](#/devices): the devices enrolled on the Hive. Enrol appears beside it on the Hive Stand.
